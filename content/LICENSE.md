## Why ShareAlike

The reference images are crops of street-level photos from [Mapillary](https://www.mapillary.com),
whose contributors publish them under CC-BY-SA 4.0. Derived works must keep the same licence, so
the whole content folder uses it.

## Attribution

Each reference image records its Mapillary image id, creator and capture date in its clue file.
`ATTRIBUTION.md` at the repository root is generated from that metadata and credits every creator.

When reusing this content, credit "Geographer contributors" and the original Mapillary creators,
link to this licence, and indicate any changes you made.

## What is not covered

Linked guide websites (listed under each clue's `sources`) are only referenced, never copied. Their
text and images remain the property of their owners.
